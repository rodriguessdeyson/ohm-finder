Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Ohm Finder mobile application (the "Application"), developed by Rad Apps (the "Service Provider"), handles information when you use the Application.

Information We Collect

Ohm Finder does not require an account, login, name, email address, contacts, password, or precise location. Calculator inputs and history are designed to remain on your device. The Application and its integrated third-party services may automatically process:

  • Approximate location: a general region inferred from an IP address, mainly for advertising, measurement, and security.
  • App interactions: screens viewed, taps, gestures, feature usage, and related interaction events.
  • Device or other identifiers: device, application, advertising, and installation identifiers used for advertising, analytics, security, and diagnostics.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: crash reports, errors, performance information, operating-system version, device model, and related technical data.

The Service Provider does not intentionally request or collect precise location, contacts, photos, passwords, financial information, or account credentials through the Application.

Analytics, Diagnostics, and Advertising Services

The Application uses the following third-party services. These providers may process information automatically according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob may collect and share IP address information, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related services. Where required, the Application uses Google's consent flow for advertising choices.
  • Microsoft Clarity for Mobile Apps is used for product analytics and troubleshooting. It may process device and application details, country or region, screens or layout state, taps, gestures, errors, and exceptions to generate session replays, heatmaps, and diagnostic insights. Clarity transmits this information to Microsoft over encrypted connections and states that it does not transfer the collected mobile data to other third parties.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase may process app usage, device, and diagnostic information for analytics and product measurement.
  • Firebase Crashlytics may process crash logs, diagnostic information, device details, and identifiers to identify and resolve crashes and stability problems.

Because Clarity can use screen and interaction information to reconstruct application sessions, do not enter information that could identify you into the Application. The Service Provider does not use Clarity to intentionally collect names, email addresses, passwords, or precise location.

How Information Is Used and Shared

Information processed by the Application and its service providers may be used for:

  • providing and improving calculations and application functionality;
  • analytics, crash diagnosis, and performance monitoring;
  • showing and measuring advertisements;
  • security, fraud prevention, and abuse prevention; and
  • complying with legal obligations.

Information may be transmitted to Google, Microsoft, Firebase, and their service providers when necessary to provide these services. The Service Provider does not sell personal information. Third-party providers may handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.
